# Technology for Life

## Learn. Explore. Connect.

**Free Computer Classes for Adults**

Technology for Life is a free, in person adult computer learning program designed to help adults learn, understand, and confidently use modern technology.

The program is built for adults of all ages, including those who remember a time when computers were not part of everyday life, those who never had the opportunity to learn, and those who simply want to continue learning.

> **It is never too late to learn.**

---

## Our Purpose

Technology for Life exists to provide adults with a welcoming, respectful environment where they can learn computer skills without embarrassment, intimidation, or judgment.

We believe that age does not determine a person's ability to learn.

Technology may change quickly, but people can continue learning throughout their lives.

Our goal is not simply to teach someone where to click.

Our goal is to help learners **understand technology well enough to use it with confidence.**

---

## Classes

Technology for Life provides:

- **Free classes**
- **In person instruction**
- **Evening classes**
- **Two hour class sessions**
- **Hands on learning**
- **Personal assistance**
- **Adult focused instruction**

Learners are expected to bring their **own laptop or desktop computer** to class.

Using their own computer allows learners to practice on the device they will actually use at home.

---

## Phase One: Desktop and Laptop Foundations

The initial program focuses exclusively on **desktop and laptop computers**.

Mobile phones and tablets are not part of Phase One because they use different interfaces and require a separate approach to instruction.

### Phase One Topics

- Understanding the computer
- Monitor and computer components
- Keyboard and mouse
- Desktop and icons
- Windows and menus
- Applications and programs
- Typing
- Files and folders
- Saving and finding files
- Downloads
- Printing
- Internet browsers
- Internet searching
- Email
- Attachments
- Basic documents
- Basic computer settings
- Software updates
- Computer safety
- Passwords
- Phishing and scams
- Online privacy
- Basic troubleshooting

The curriculum will be designed to move from fundamental concepts toward practical everyday computer use.

---

## Phase Two: Mobile Technology

Mobile technology will be developed as a **separate learning opportunity** after the desktop and laptop foundation has been established.

Phase Two may include:

- Smartphones
- Tablets
- Touchscreen navigation
- Mobile applications
- Mobile communication
- Mobile photography
- Mobile settings
- Mobile safety
- Everyday smartphone functions

The desktop foundation comes first.

**Desktop first. Mobile second.**

The purpose is to help learners understand technology as a system rather than simply memorizing where individual buttons are located.

---

## Our Learning Philosophy

Technology for Life is learner centered.

We do not expect every learner to move at the same speed.

Some people will understand a concept immediately.

Others may need additional demonstrations, explanations, or practice.

That is okay.

### There Are No Stupid Questions

If something needs to be explained three times, we explain it three times.

If something needs to be practiced ten times, we practice it ten times.

Learning is not a race.

The goal is for every learner to leave class thinking:

> **I can do this.**

---

## A Typical Two Hour Class

A class may include:

### Review and Questions

Review previous material and address questions from earlier lessons.

### New Lesson

Introduce and demonstrate a new computer skill.

### Hands On Practice

Learners practice the new skill on their own computers.

### Individual Assistance

Provide additional help, troubleshooting, and one on one guidance.

### Review

Summarize the lesson and identify skills to practice before the next class.

The exact structure may change as the program develops.

---

## Who Is This For?

Technology for Life welcomes adults across generations.

It is for the middle aged adult who remembers life before computers became common.

It is for the older adult who wants to learn something they never had the opportunity to learn.

It is for the adult who can use a computer but wants greater confidence.

It is for someone who is beginning from the very beginning.

It is for the person who simply wants to keep their mind active by learning something new.

**You do not have to be young to learn technology.**

You do not have to be naturally good with computers.

You do not have to be afraid of making mistakes.

You simply have to be willing to learn.
