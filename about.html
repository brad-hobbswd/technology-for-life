ABOUT TECHNOLOGY FOR LIFE

Learning does not have an expiration date.

We believe every adult deserves the opportunity to understand technology without feeling embarrassed, rushed, or left behind.

WHY WE EXIST

Technology changed. The desire to learn did not.

There are adults who remember when a computer was something you saw in an office, a classroom, or a television show rather than something sitting in every home. There are others who simply never had the opportunity to learn how computers work.

Technology for Life was created for them and for anyone else who wants to learn. We provide a place where adults can ask questions, practice new skills, make mistakes, and try again without being judged.

We are not interested in making learners feel behind. We are interested in helping them move forward.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

A different kind of computer class.

01

Respect

Adults deserve to be taught as adults. We never use childish language or assume that age limits a person's ability to understand.

02

Patience

People learn at different speeds. We make room for repetition, questions, practice, and individual assistance.

03

Confidence

The measure of success is not how quickly someone finishes a lesson. It is whether they leave knowing they can do it.

04

Understanding

We teach concepts instead of asking learners to memorize a collection of buttons and instructions.

FOR EVERY LEARNER

You belong here.

Maybe you have never owned a computer. Maybe you have one at home but are afraid to click the wrong thing. Maybe you can browse the Internet but still do not understand files, folders, email, or settings.

Wherever you are starting, that is where we begin.

Our promise

“There are no stupid questions here.”

If you need it explained again, we explain it again. If you need more practice, we practice. Learning is not a race.

DEVICE SUPPORT

Bring the laptop you actually use.

Windows laptops are the primary platform for Technology for Life. Windows is the platform on which instruction is strongest and most detailed.

Chromebooks are also welcome. The program includes support for ChromeOS, Google tools, web browsing, email, online safety, and other browser based skills.

Mac laptops may be used, but support is more limited. General computing concepts may transfer, but Mac specific instruction is not currently a primary focus.

Desktop computers are not permitted for in person training because they are not practical to transport. If instruction is offered through Microsoft Teams, learners may use a desktop computer from home.

Wi-Fi will be available at the training location once an in person training site has been established.

THE ROAD AHEAD

Laptop first. Mobile second.

Our first phase focuses on laptop computers because they provide a strong foundation for understanding how digital technology works while remaining practical for learners to bring to class.

Mobile phones and tablets will have their own separate learning opportunity. Once learners understand the fundamentals of computing, they can carry that knowledge into other interfaces and devices.
