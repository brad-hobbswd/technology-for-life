THE CLASSROOM

Come ready to learn. Leave ready to try.

Free, in person computer classes designed around adults, real questions, hands on practice, and the pace of the people in the room.

CLASS ESSENTIALS

Simple expectations. Plenty of support.

We keep the requirements straightforward so learners can focus on learning.

01

Free

There is no tuition or class fee. Learning should be accessible to adults who want the opportunity.

02

In Person

Instruction happens face to face so learners can ask questions and receive personal assistance.

03

Two Hours

Each evening session provides time for instruction, practice, questions, and review without rushing.

04

Bring Your Laptop

Bring your own Windows laptop or Chromebook so the skills you practice are skills you can use at home. Desktop computers are not permitted for in person training because they are not practical to transport.

EVENING LEARNING

A place to learn after the day is done.

Classes are offered in the evening to make participation easier for adults who have work, family, or other daytime responsibilities.

The class is not built around how quickly an instructor can finish a lesson. It is built around whether the learner has had enough time to understand and practice the skill.

TWO HOURS TO LEARN

What a class can look like

01

Review and Questions

We begin by revisiting previous skills and answering questions from earlier lessons.

02

New Lesson

The instructor introduces and demonstrates a new computer skill step by step.

03

Hands On Practice

Learners practice the new skill on their own computers while the instructor provides guidance.

04

Individual Assistance

Time is available for troubleshooting, repetition, and questions that are specific to each learner.

05

Review

We close by reviewing what was learned and identifying useful practice before the next class.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Bring the laptop you use.

Windows laptops are the primary platform for Phase One instruction. Chromebooks are also welcome, especially for learning web browsing, Google tools, email, online safety, and other browser based skills. Mac laptops may be used, but Mac specific instruction is currently limited. Desktop computers are not permitted for in person training because they are not practical to transport. If instruction is offered through Microsoft Teams, a desktop computer may be used from home.

REMEMBER

You do not have to know everything before you begin.
