BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

You do not need the right words.

You can simply tell us what you are trying to do, what you are having trouble with, or what you want to learn. We can start there.

“I have never used a computer.”That is okay. The program starts with foundations.
“I am afraid I will mess something up.”That is exactly why practice and support are part of class.
“I am not sure my computer is right.”Ask us and we can help you understand what you have.
“I have a question I feel embarrassed to ask.”Ask it anyway. There are no stupid questions here.
