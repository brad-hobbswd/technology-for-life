FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

It is okay to ask.

Here are some of the questions learners may have before beginning Technology for Life.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Start with what you want to know.

Who are these classes for?

Technology for Life is designed for adult learners, including people who grew up before computers were part of everyday life and older adults who want to continue learning new technology skills.

Do I need computer experience?

No. Phase One is designed to establish the foundations, so you can begin even if you have very little or no computer experience.

How much do classes cost?

Classes are free. There is no tuition or class fee for learners.

Are classes online?

Phase One classes are in person. The first learning pathway focuses on desktop and laptop computers. Mobile technology will have a separate opportunity later.

What kind of computer should I bring?

A Windows laptop or desktop is the best supported option for Phase One instruction. Chromebooks are also welcome, especially for ChromeOS, Google tools, web browsing, email, online safety, and other browser based skills. Mac computers have limited support at this time, so Mac specific instruction is not currently a primary focus.

Can I bring a Chromebook?

Yes. Chromebooks are welcome. The instructor is comfortable working with ChromeOS and Google based tools, and many foundational skills transfer well to Chromebook use.

Can I bring a Mac?

You may bring a Mac, but Mac specific instruction is currently limited. Technology for Life's strongest hands on instruction is on Windows computers, while Chromebook support is also available.

How long is each class?

Each evening class is designed to last two hours, including instruction, practice, questions, review, and individual support.

What do I need to bring?

You need to bring your own Windows laptop, Windows desktop, or Chromebook for Phase One classes. Bringing your own device lets you practice on the computer you use at home.

What if I am afraid of making a mistake?

Mistakes are part of learning. The classroom is designed to give you a safe place to practice, ask questions, and try again.

What if everyone else learns faster than I do?

Learning is not a race. We make room for different learning speeds, repetition, questions, and individual assistance.

Will I be tested before I can attend?

No entrance test is required. You do not have to prove what you already know before you begin.

Will there be classes for phones and tablets?

Mobile devices are planned as a separate phase. Desktop and laptop fundamentals come first because they provide a strong foundation for understanding computing.

When will classes begin?

The first class dates, location, and registration details will be posted on the Schedule and Registration pages once they are established.

What if I have a question that is not listed here?

Please reach out. Questions are welcome, and you do not need to know the right computer terms to ask for help.

ONE MORE THING

You belong in the classroom.

You are never too old to learn something new, and you are never too inexperienced to begin.
