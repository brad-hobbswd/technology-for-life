HELPFUL WEBSITES

Good places to keep learning.

The internet is full of information. These resources give you a starting place with organizations and services that are useful, familiar, or designed to help people learn.

BEFORE YOU CLICK

A search result is not automatically trustworthy.

Before entering personal information, downloading something, or sending money, take a moment to make sure you know who operates the website and why you are there.

When possible, type a known website address yourself or use a trusted bookmark instead of following an unexpected link.

CHOOSE CAREFULLY

Ask three questions before trusting a website.

Who runs this website?Look for the organization behind the information.
Why am I being asked for this information?Do not share personal information without understanding why it is needed.
Did I choose to come here?An unexpected link or message deserves extra caution.
