UPCOMING COMPUTER CLASSES

Beginner computer lessons for everyday life.

Whether you are in midlife, retired, or simply ready to learn, you do not need previous computer experience. Our beginner computer classes help adults practice practical skills, understand the technology used in daily life, and feel more confident online.

WELCOME

Learn without intimidation.

Technology may have changed quickly, but learning does not have an expiration date. Whether you remember a time before computers were part of everyday life or you simply never had the opportunity to learn, you belong here.

Our classes are designed for adults of all ages. We move at the pace of the learners, provide hands on practice, and make room for questions, repetition, and individual assistance.

PHASE ONE

Laptop Foundations

Phase One focuses on laptop computers. Windows laptops are the primary platform for instruction, and Chromebooks are also welcome. Mac laptops may be used, but Mac specific instruction is more limited. Desktop computers are not permitted for in person training because they are not practical to transport. If a class is offered through Microsoft Teams, learners may use a desktop computer from home.

01

Understand

Learn what the computer, keyboard, mouse, desktop, windows, programs, files, and folders actually do.

02

Practice

Bring your own Windows laptop or Chromebook and practice each skill on the computer you will use at home.

03

Build Confidence

Develop practical skills for everyday computer use while learning how to recognize and solve common problems.

WHAT TO EXPECT

A class built around the learner.

Each meeting lasts two hours. A typical learning session is planned for at least six weeks, meeting one day each week. This is a minimum framework. Some studies may take less time, while hands on studies may require additional weeks for practice, repetition, troubleshooting, and individual support.

Active learner participation is required. Every learner is expected to attend, pay attention, practice the skills being taught, ask questions when something is unclear, make a genuine effort to learn, and contribute positively to the classroom environment.

Classes are free. Financial donations are welcome when a learner is able and chooses to give, but donating money is never required for registration, attendance, or participation.

WHO THESE CLASSES ARE FOR

Start learning at any age.

Technology for Life welcomes middle aged adults, older adults, retirees, and anyone who missed the chance to learn computer skills earlier in life. You do not need to feel embarrassed about starting with the basics. We begin with the essentials, explain unfamiliar terms in plain language, and make time for questions and repeated practice.

Whether your goal is to send an email, search the web, organize files, join an online meeting, or better understand online scams, learning one skill at a time can make everyday technology feel more manageable.
