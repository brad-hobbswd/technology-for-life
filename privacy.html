PRIVACY

Your information deserves care.

This page explains the privacy principles Technology for Life intends to follow as the program and website develop.

OUR APPROACH

Keep information limited, useful, and protected.

Technology for Life is designed as a free educational program. We intend to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to communicate with learners, organize classes, provide services, or maintain the website.

Information You Provide

If you contact the program or register for a class, you may provide information such as your name, contact information, class preferences, or other information needed to participate.

How Information Is Used

Information provided to Technology for Life should be used for program communication, registration, scheduling, class organization, learner support, and other purposes directly related to operating the program.

Information Sharing

Personal information should not be sold or shared for advertising purposes. Information may need to be shared with service providers when necessary to operate a registration, communication, hosting, or other website function.

Website Services

The website may eventually use third party services for forms, hosting, analytics, registration, or other functions. When those services are added, this policy should be updated to explain their role.

Children

Technology for Life is designed for adult learners. The program is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through registration or program services.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy page may be updated as the website and program develop. The current version published on this page should be treated as the applicable version for the website.

Important: This is a foundational website privacy statement and should be reviewed and updated when the actual registration, communication, analytics, and hosting services are finalized.

QUESTIONS

Privacy questions are welcome.

If you have questions about how information is handled, please contact Technology for Life.
