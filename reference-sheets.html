REFERENCE SHEETS

Keep the basics close by.

These quick reference sheets are designed to sit beside your computer while you learn. Use them as reminders, not as a test.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Shortcuts worth remembering

These common shortcuts can make everyday computer tasks easier. On many Windows computers, the Ctrl key is used with another key.

Ctrl + CCopy
Ctrl + VPaste
Ctrl + XCut
Ctrl + ZUndo
Ctrl + SSave
Ctrl + ASelect all
Ctrl + FFind
Ctrl + TNew browser tab
Ctrl + WClose the current tab or window
Alt + TabSwitch between open windows

COMPUTER TERMS

Words you will see often

Desktop

The main workspace you see after your computer starts.

Window

The area where a program or website is displayed.

Browser

A program used to visit websites, such as Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Firefox.

Search Engine

A service that helps you find information on the internet.

File

A saved item on your computer, such as a document, photograph, or PDF.

Folder

A place used to organize and store files.

Download

To move or save something from the internet onto your computer.

Upload

To send a file or other information from your computer to a website or service.

Link

A clickable item that takes you to another webpage, file, or location.

Tab

A separate page or workspace within a browser window.

App

A software program designed to perform a task.

Wi Fi

A wireless connection that can allow your computer to access a network or the internet.

QUICK REMINDERS

Keep these beside your computer

SAVE YOUR WORK

Save before you close a document or program.

READ BEFORE YOU CLICK

Take a moment to understand what a link or button will do.

KEEP PASSWORDS PRIVATE

Do not give your passwords to someone simply because they ask.

STOP IF YOU ARE UNSURE

You can always pause and ask for help.

TAKE YOUR TIME

There is no race. Slow and steady is still learning.

MISTAKES ARE OKAY

A mistake can show you what to practice next.
