REGISTRATION

Your next step can be the first step.

Registration is designed to be simple. We want you thinking about what you will learn, not worrying about whether you know enough to begin.

START HERE

You do not need computer experience to register.

Technology for Life welcomes adults at different levels of computer experience. You do not need to be an expert, and you do not need to understand computer terminology before attending.

Our first phase is designed to build from the beginning, so learners can establish a strong foundation before moving into more advanced everyday skills.

BEFORE YOU REGISTER

What you need to participate

01

Be an Adult Learner

The program is designed for adults who want to learn or strengthen their computer skills.

02

Bring Your Computer

A laptop or desktop computer is required for Phase One classes.

03

Come Ready to Learn

You do not need to know everything. Curiosity and a willingness to practice are enough.

04

Attend In Person

Classes are taught face to face so learners can receive hands on support.

THE PROCESS

Registration should feel easy.

Specific registration instructions will be added when the first class session is scheduled.

01

Choose a Class

Review the available evening sessions and select the one that works for you.

02

Register

Complete the simple registration process for your selected session.

03

Prepare

Bring your laptop or desktop computer and any questions you want help with.

04

Come Learn

Arrive ready to learn at your own pace in a supportive classroom.

NO TEST REQUIRED

You are not being tested before you begin.

There is no entrance exam. There is no need to prove that you already understand computers. We meet you where you are and build from there.

Your willingness to learn is enough.

REGISTRATION APPLICATION

Download the fillable registration application.

Complete the application on your computer. You can type directly into the form fields, save the completed PDF, and keep it ready for submission when registration opens.

The completed application will be submitted to the Technology for Life webmaster through the registration submission process.
