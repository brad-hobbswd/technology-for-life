LEARNING RESOURCES

Practice does not have to end when class does.

Resources give learners a place to review what they learned, practice at home, and keep building confidence between classes.

KEEP LEARNING

Resources should make learning easier, not more complicated.

We are building this library around the same principles used in class. Materials are clear, practical, and written for adults who are learning computer skills.

The library will grow with the program and provide printable guides, practice exercises, lesson reviews, helpful websites, and other materials that support independent learning.

PRACTICE AT HOME

Your own computer is your practice space.

Because learners bring their own computers to class, the skills they practice can continue at home on the same device.

Try it againRepeat the skill until it feels familiar.
Write it downKeep notes in your own words.
Ask questionsBring anything confusing back to class.
Do not be afraidMaking a mistake is part of learning.

FOR LEARNERS

You are allowed to take your time.

Technology can be learned one skill at a time. You do not have to understand everything at once, and you do not have to compare your progress with anyone else.
