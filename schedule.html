CLASS SCHEDULE

Make time to learn something new.

Technology for Life classes are designed for adults who want a welcoming opportunity to learn computer skills at a pace that allows time for instruction, hands on practice, questions, and review.

OUR CLASS FORMAT

One evening a week. Two hours at a time.

A typical learning session is planned for at least six weeks, meeting one day each week for two hours. This is a minimum framework rather than a rigid finish date. Individual studies may take less or more time depending on the topic, the amount of hands on work, repetition, questions, and individual support learners need.

SESSION

At Least 6 Weeks

Each learning session is planned for a minimum of six weekly meetings.

WEEKLY FORMAT

One Day a Week

Classes meet once each week so learners have time to practice between sessions.

CLASS LENGTH

Two Hours

Each meeting allows time for instruction, hands on practice, questions, review, and individual assistance.

LOCATION

In Person

Classes are taught face to face when a training location is available. Wi-Fi will be available once an in person training site has been established.

LEARNING AT THE RIGHT PACE

Not every study takes the same amount of time.

Some topics can be introduced and practiced within a shorter period. Other studies require more hands on work, repetition, troubleshooting, or individual assistance. The six week minimum gives the program room to teach foundational skills without treating every learner or every topic as if it moves at the same speed.

MINIMUM SESSION FORMAT6 Weeks • 1 Day Each Week • 2 Hours

Additional time may be added when a study benefits from more practice and support.

ANOTHER KIND OF CONTRIBUTION

The classes are free, but learning is worth investing in.

Technology for Life does not charge tuition for learners. Because the program is offered at no cost, we encourage each learner to contribute in a way that is meaningful and possible for them.

Your contribution may be your time, attention, willingness to practice, patience, encouragement, or ability to help others learn. If you are able and would like to provide a financial donation, that is welcome too. No learner is required to donate money or volunteer in order to participate.

The most important contribution is showing up, being willing to learn, and making the most of the opportunity.

A TWO HOUR LEARNING EVENING

Every class has room to breathe.

01

Arrive and Settle In

Bring your Windows laptop or Chromebook, get comfortable, and prepare for the evening.

02

Review

Reconnect with skills from previous lessons and ask questions.

03

Learn Something New

Follow a clear demonstration and learn the concept behind the skill.

04

Practice

Work through the skill on your own laptop with support available.

05

Review and Go Home Ready

Finish with a clear understanding of what you learned and what you can practice next.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST CLASS

Bring your laptop and bring your questions.

A Windows laptop or Chromebook is required for Phase One in person classes. Desktop computers are not permitted for in person training because they are not practical to transport. If instruction is offered through Microsoft Teams, a desktop computer may be used from home.
