A LEARNING EVENING

Every class has room to breathe.

01

Arrive and Settle In

Bring your computer, get comfortable, and prepare for the evening.

02

Review

Reconnect with skills from previous lessons and ask questions.

03

Learn Something New

Follow a clear demonstration and learn the concept behind the skill.

04

Practice

Work through the skill on your own computer with support available.

05

Review and Go Home Ready

Finish with a clear understanding of what you learned and what you can practice next.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST CLASS

Bring your computer and bring your questions.

A laptop or desktop computer is required for Phase One. You do not need to know how to use it perfectly before you arrive.
