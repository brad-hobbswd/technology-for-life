TERMS AND GUIDELINES

Learn together. Respect one another.

These guidelines describe the intended use of the Technology for Life website and the expectations for participating in the program.

USING THIS WEBSITE

A place for learning and information.

Educational Purpose

Technology for Life provides educational information and resources intended to support adult computer learning. Website materials are educational resources and are not a substitute for professional legal, financial, medical, or other specialized advice.

Website Content

We will make reasonable efforts to keep information accurate and useful. Class dates, availability, locations, resources, and other program details may change as the program develops.

Learning Resources

Resources provided through this website are intended to support practice and learning. Learners are responsible for using care when downloading files, visiting external websites, entering personal information, or changing settings on their own devices.

Respectful Classroom

Participants are expected to treat instructors, volunteers, and fellow learners with patience and respect. Technology for Life is intended to be a welcoming learning environment for adults at different experience levels.

Personal Devices

Phase One learners bring their own laptop or desktop computer. Technology for Life cannot guarantee compatibility with every device, operating system, software version, or configuration.

External Websites

The website may eventually include links to third party websites. Technology for Life does not control those websites and is not responsible for their content, availability, privacy practices, or terms.

Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as the website and program develop. The current version published on this page should be treated as the applicable version for website use.

Important: These are foundational website and program guidelines. They should be reviewed and updated when the program's legal structure, registration process, classroom policies, and technology services are finalized.

LEARNING TOGETHER

Respect makes room for everyone.

Come ready to learn, ask questions, practice, and encourage the people learning beside you.
